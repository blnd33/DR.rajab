# Phase 4 — Money · Plan

> Status: **Built and verified** (2026-10-26) — straight after Phase 3 (the client asked on 2026-10-26 to run the remaining phases back-to-back); this plan records the scope and the decisions taken. What changed from the plan: see CHANGELOG § Phase 4.
> Brief scope: *Currencies & rates, cash boxes + daily closing, currency exchange, suppliers & purchases & payments & landed costs & approvals, sales & invoices, expenses, (commissions if confirmed).* Modules 7, 13, 19, 20, 21, 22 (+ 3.3 money rules, 14 credit notes, 26 money cards).
> Tables: [DATABASE.md](../DATABASE.md) § Phase 4.

## 1. Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| P4-1 | **Money math** | `App\Support\Money\Money` (brick/math `BigDecimal`, scale 4, HALF_UP) + `FxConverter`: converts any amount between USD / EUR / IQD with **one `fx_snapshots` row** (USD→IQD and EUR→USD). Never floats. |
| P4-2 | **Rates** | `exchange_rates` is append-only. USD/IQD is entered by a permitted user (market rate); EUR/USD can be entered or fetched daily from the ECB (free, no key) as a *suggestion* — confirm by hand, or automatically when the setting says so. Confirming creates a new immutable `fx_snapshots` row (= "current"). Money documents can't be saved before the first snapshot exists ("Set the exchange rates first"). |
| P4-3 | **Money ledger** | `MoneyLedgerService` is the only writer of `money_transactions` and `cash_boxes.current_balance`: transaction + `lockForUpdate` on the box, signed amount in the box currency, `amount_usd` through the snapshot, `balance_after`. A cash box can't go below zero (bank accounts may, when the box allows overdraft). Corrections are reversing rows (`reverses_id`). Append-only (trigger + model guard), like `party_ledger_entries`. |
| P4-4 | **Cash boxes** | Per branch and currency (`cash`) plus bank accounts (`bank`); head-office boxes have no branch. Opening balance is a ledger row. |
| P4-5 | **Daily closing** | The cashier counts each box (optional denominations) → expected vs counted → a difference needs a reason → *submit* locks the closing and posts the difference as a `closing_adjustment` (so the box matches the drawer) and opens a *cash difference* issue. Reviewed by `cash_boxes.review_closing`. One closing per box per day. |
| P4-6 | **Box transfers & exchange** | Same-currency transfer between boxes / branches: *send* posts the out leg, *receive* the in leg, *cancel* reverses the out leg. Currency exchange (own money only): out of one box, into another at the deal rate; gain / loss vs the snapshot's reference rate kept in USD. |
| P4-7 | **Purchases** | Supplier invoice (number unique per supplier, dates, currency, receiving branch, lines product × qty × price, attachment) draft → *confirm* (a payable on the supplier's ledger) → receive in one or more receipts (scan GS1 / serial / lot or type; serial, lot, expiry per line) through `StockMovementService` (`purchase_receipt`) with the true cost. Stock rows remember their `purchase_item_id`; the lot bucket key includes it, so each purchase keeps its own cost layer. Cancel only before anything is received or paid. |
| P4-8 | **Landed costs** | Shipping / customs / clearance / bank fees on a purchase, optional payee (freight forwarder → their payable), allocated by value or quantity to the lines → per-unit landed cost in the purchase currency → `cost_adjustment` on the units / lots still in stock (true cost = base + landed); quantities already used or sold are recorded as a cost-of-sales adjustment on the allocation. |
| P4-9 | **Supplier payments** | From a cash box / bank account (box currency), method (bank wire, hawala, cash, cheque, other), reference, date, receipt; allocated to the supplier's open purchases (oldest due first by default) — a payment in another currency converts at the payment rate (stored) and records the realized exchange gain / loss vs the invoice snapshot. Above the threshold (setting, in USD) → *pending approval* for `supplier_payments.approve`; posting happens on approval. |
| P4-10 | **Party ledger** | `party_ledger_entries` (append-only, per currency) for suppliers and customers: invoice, payment, credit note, landed cost, reversal. Statement page per supplier / customer with the balance per currency; due-date list + daily overdue alert. |
| P4-11 | **Approvals** | One `approvals` queue (supplier payments, expenses) with approve / reject from the dashboard and the queue screen; write-offs and stock requests keep their own flows (Phase 2) and are counted in the same dashboard card. |
| P4-12 | **Sales** | Cashier (phone or admin) scans units / lots, picks a hospital / customer or walk-in, currency, price (product price converted through the snapshot), discount; paid now into a cash box. Credit sales (open question 2) stay behind the *credit sales* feature flag (off: full payment required). Issue → stock `sale` + money `sale_receipt`; *void* (`sales.void`, reason) reverses both. Printed invoice with the Phase 5 PDFs. |
| P4-13 | **Customer payments & credit notes** | Only with credit sales on: payments allocated to open invoices. Credit notes from suppliers come from Phase 2 supplier returns (*Record credit*), to customers from customer returns of sold items. |
| P4-14 | **Expenses** | Categories (translatable, seeded); expense from a box (branch or head office), category, payee, receipt; above the threshold → approval; general expenses can be allocated across branches (percentages = 100). Recurring expenses (monthly, day of month) generated daily by the scheduler (posted or pending per rule). |
| P4-15 | **Commissions** | Built behind the *commissions* feature flag (open question 5): rules per doctor / surgery type / product (fixed or %), accrued when a surgery completes, approved and paid from a box. Screens hidden while the flag is off. |
| P4-16 | **Old documents get their snapshot** | `fx_snapshot_id` columns added to write-offs, supplier returns, surgeries (fee) and stock cost; write-off cost in USD filled from now on. Stock value on the dashboard shows one total in USD (plus per currency). |
| P4-17 | **Phone (cashier)** | *Cash* tab inside Tasks: boxes and balances, new sale (scan), expense, currency exchange, daily closing. |
