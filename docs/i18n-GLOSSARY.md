# Glossary — one word per business term

The Arabic and Sorani words the app uses for each business term, taken from `lang/ar.json` and `lang/ckb.json`.
Use the same word in every new string. Codes (SKU, GTIN, IBAN, serial and lot numbers, document numbers) stay in Latin letters and Western digits.
Written by the developer — a native-speaker review is still recommended before go-live; change a term here and in the language files together.
